# Mechanics Laboratory

The HTML builds are frozen mechanics and interaction references. Unity is the production track.

## Preserved builds

- `SevereWeather_MechanicsLab_v0.7.1.html`: larger transition-era baseline
- `SevereWeather_MechanicsLab_v0.8.0.html`: later compact experiment; its internal page title says `v1.3.5`

The newer experiment includes Tornado, Supercell, Derecho, EF score scaling, dynamic zoom, NOAA/EAS framing, power-grid cascades, chaser vans, landmarks, and invincible animals. These are prototype explorations, not proof of equivalent Unity integration.

## Known limitations

- environment art is not production quality
- map density is inconsistent
- storm physics and abilities share more generic logic than the production design permits
- Supercell and Derecho do not yet meet full production differentiation
- impact audio is overly synthetic or absent
- the Canvas renderer is not the production renderer
- the single-file architecture is not a production architecture

Use these builds to compare control ideas, ability timing, mission pacing, tone, and presentation experiments. Do not continue investing in their environment-rendering pipeline.
