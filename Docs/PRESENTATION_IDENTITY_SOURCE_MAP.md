# Presentation Identity Source Map

## Purpose

This pass restores the comic identity promised by the accepted Severe Weather Warning design without changing scoring, abilities, campaign timing, destruction laws, or the green city-fabric baseline.

## Canonical opening contract

The startup sequence follows the project record:

1. warning newspaper tumbles into view
2. the paper peels away to reveal Hart Farm
3. Cow 17 drinks Moo Brew
4. the radio and weather change
5. Cow 17 performs a double take
6. the cup drops logo-forward and chickens scatter
7. the barn roof peels and the tornado touches down
8. the camera treatment rises toward the tactical view
9. the existing Start button resumes the accepted live game

The sequence is skippable. Automated QA bypasses it unless `intro=1` is explicitly requested.

## Cow readability contract

Every live cow uses a readable low-poly rig attached to the accepted bovine gameplay object. The rig includes:

- broad body and shoulders
- distinct head and muzzle
- eyes, ears, horns, and nostrils
- four legs and four hooves
- udder, tail, and black-and-white markings
- expressive leg, ear, head, and tail movement driven by the accepted bovine update
- a readable Cow 17 identity tag

The pass does not change safe-animal behavior, collision truth, launch rules, airtime accounting, or Cow-Cam telemetry.

## Moo Brew identity contract

Moo Brew appears in three layers:

- the canonical startup broadcast
- non-target billboards and delivery trucks rebuilt through the accepted county lifecycle
- a bounded score-screen sponsor panel and small broadcast bug

Environmental sponsor props are scenery only. They do not enter the target array, score system, objectives, or destruction counts.

## Results-screen containment contract

The score card keeps its title, grade, and action buttons fixed inside the viewport. Detailed statistics, the Bovine Situation Report, campaign result, and sponsor panel occupy one bounded internal scroll region.

The QA matrix explicitly checks:

- 1365 × 630 landscape
- 932 × 430 mobile landscape
- 760 × 360 compact landscape

At each size the card, title, and actions must remain fully inside the viewport.

## Protected boundaries

This pass does not alter:

- Three.js version
- road or city-fabric generation
- scoring or combo laws
- campaign progression
- Power or Stability
- abilities or input routing
- destruction-state transitions
- Phase 6 performance contracts
- safe-animal law
- historical source provenance

Physical Android acceptance remains owner-controlled.
