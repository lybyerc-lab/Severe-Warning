# Light Action RPG Progression Task Seed

Queued design seed. Do not implement until the Director assigns an exact base after GAME-002 and the first Stage 2B playtest evidence.

## Goal

Restore the intended light-action-RPG progression layer without burying the arcade destruction game under grind.

## Required first proof

- persistent local-first earned soft currency;
- working currency name **MOO-LAH** with cow-face / udder coin identity;
- a small upgrade screen that makes at least Pull, Gust, and Grid Zap visibly upgradeable;
- base abilities remain fun and complete;
- upgrades are bounded, meaningful, and understandable;
- no real-money economy by default;
- exact before/after tuning and regression evidence;
- results/newspaper surfaces can communicate earned currency and available upgrades.

See `Docs/LEVEL_AND_RPG_EXPANSION.md` for full product law.
