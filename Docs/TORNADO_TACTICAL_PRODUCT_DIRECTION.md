# Tornado Tactical Product Direction

Status: LOCKED PRODUCT DIRECTION
Decision date: 2026-07-29
Authoritative gameplay reference: `MechanicsLab/SevereWeather_3D_Lab.html` at HTML Lab v2.5.0
Reference commit: `9b856fd2586bc0c70e3ee95da02fe1dfe0162f74`
Production engine: Unity 6000.3.0f1

## Core decision

The HTML 2.5 Tactical build is now the authoritative gameplay, comfort, layout, pacing, and enjoyment reference for the production game.

Unity remains the production engine, but Unity must reproduce and improve the HTML experience rather than inventing a separate game around the existing Build 5.2 debug slice.

The product rule is:

> Unity must feel at least as comfortable, readable, destructive, and enjoyable as HTML 2.5 before the project adds more storm classes or wider scope.

## Primary vertical slice

The first polished production vertical slice is Tornado only.

Supercell and Derecho remain preserved in source and design notes, but they do not control the first production milestone. Tornado is the strongest current gameplay experience and receives the first complete pass.

The vertical slice must include:

- pulled-back tactical camera with movement look-ahead
- comfortable mobile joystick movement
- Tornado Pull, Gust, and Grid Zap
- generous, readable destruction
- four recognizable districts
- score and combo systems
- EF growth and visible tornado escalation
- three-minute run timer
- objectives, landmark tracking, and results screen
- persistent warning/news ticker
- breaking-news callouts driven by real gameplay events
- active storm chaser crews and captured-on-camera bonuses
- invincible, flingable animals with safe landings
- opening broadcast and ending news recap
- strong landscape, lighting, atmosphere, audio, and aftermath

## Non-negotiable fun laws

1. The player is the storm.
2. Direct action comes before management.
3. The tactical camera must reveal opportunities rather than conceal them.
4. Destruction must be generous and immediately readable.
5. The player must encounter valuable targets quickly.
6. Tornado growth must be visible, audible, and mechanical.
7. People are protected, off-limits, and never casualties.
8. Animals are invincible and may be lifted, orbited, flung, and safely landed.
9. Storm chasers are witnesses and moving camera zones, not attack targets.
10. The news system reports actual events generated by play.
11. Graphics must strengthen gameplay readability, power, scale, and aftermath.
12. Unity compilation alone does not satisfy a milestone. Physical-device enjoyment is authoritative.

## Target emotional rhythm

A run should feel like this:

1. A brief broadcast warns that the storm is touching down.
2. The player immediately sees nearby destructible opportunities.
3. Pull creates orbiting debris and lifts light targets and animals.
4. Gust throws captured objects outward and opens the landscape.
5. Grid Zap creates a readable electrical cascade.
6. Score, combo, EF level, ticker, and chaser reactions confirm the event.
7. Larger landmarks and denser districts become visible as the storm grows.
8. The run ends with a local-news-style recap of what actually happened.

## Four-district county

### Downtown

- glass, signs, rooftops, utility clutter, dense combo routes
- compact landmarks visible from the tactical camera

### Suburbs

- houses, fences, trees, sheds, vehicles, yards
- modular roof and wall failures

### Industrial

- warehouses, tanks, substations, power poles, heavy debris
- electrical cascades and large staged failures

### Farmland

- crops, barns, silos, farm equipment, fences, animals
- broad visible tornado paths and flattened aftermath

The county must remain readable. Large empty travel zones are not acceptable merely to make the map look bigger.

## Flingable animal rules

Animals are never harmed and are never counted as victims.

Required species profiles:

- Cow: heavy, slow orbit, strong launch impact
- Pig: medium weight, fast rolling spin
- Sheep: light, floaty, soft bounce
- Chicken: very light, chaotic flight path

Required interactions:

- gradual lift during Pull
- visible orbit state
- Gust launch with controlled trajectory
- harmless collisions with buildings, signs, hay bales, and landmarks
- safe landing recovery
- automatic safe reset if lost outside the play area
- species-specific audio and animation

Possible gameplay callouts include `BOVINE BALLISTIC`, `FOWL WEATHER`, `PORKNADO`, `SHEEP SHOT`, and `BARNYARD ORBIT`.

## News ticker and broadcast rules

The top warning ticker is a core player-facing system, not decorative UI.

It should report actual run events such as:

- warning issuance and district movement
- EF upgrades
- landmark destruction
- power flashes and grid cascades
- large debris events
- animals reported airborne
- storm chaser footage moments

A shared event pipeline should feed scoring, objectives, the ticker, chaser reactions, and the results recap. Systems must not separately guess what happened.

Recommended architecture:

- `StormEventBus`
- `NewsTickerController`
- `BreakingNewsController`
- `StormChaserDirector`
- `BroadcastCommentarySystem`
- `RunEventRecorder`
- `ResultsNewsReport`

## Storm chaser rules

Storm chasers make the tornado feel observed and important.

Required first-slice behavior:

- one detailed chase SUV and one secondary vehicle
- road-following movement
- safe observation positions
- camera or drone deployment
- automatic early retreat when the storm approaches
- radio captions and audio reactions
- no vulnerable people in the play space
- no score for targeting chasers
- no collision or destruction objective involving chasers

Destruction inside a chaser camera cone may receive a modest `CAUGHT ON CAMERA` bonus. This creates temporary performance opportunities without filling the landscape with artificial rings.

## Graphics direction

Phenomenal graphics means visual systems that communicate gameplay:

- readable tornado silhouette at tactical distance
- layered condensation and debris circulation
- animated cloud shadows
- crops and trees reacting before impact
- modular roof and wall failures
- power flashes moving through utility networks
- rain curtains, dust sheets, and depth cues
- persistent wreckage and flattened paths
- district-specific materials and silhouettes
- impact-scaled camera response
- powerful storm audio and material-specific destruction sounds

Cinematic effects must not hide targets, controls, the tornado, or its influence area.

## Production order

1. Capture the current repaired Unity build result.
2. Preserve the Build 5.2 foundation.
3. Match HTML 2.5 tactical camera and movement.
4. Match Tornado ability comfort and destructive generosity.
5. Add the arcade run loop.
6. Rebuild the county around four readable districts.
7. Add flingable animals, news, and storm chasers as core systems.
8. Elevate graphics, audio, physics, and aftermath.
9. Add other storms only after Tornado parity and device performance pass.

## No-drift gate

Any proposal that changes the camera, slows access to destruction, removes the ticker, removes storm chasers, removes flingable animals, or shifts focus away from Tornado must explicitly explain how it improves physical-device enjoyment relative to HTML 2.5.

Absent that evidence, this document wins.
